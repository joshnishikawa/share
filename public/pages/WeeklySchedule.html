WEEKLY SCHEDULE

An activity for practicing weekdays and interests. These cards can be used in various ways.

Basic Group Practice
  1. Get into groups of 4 or 5.
  2. Give each student a card with a schedule.
  3. Display one of the weekdays.
  4. Each student in the group should take a turn saying, eg. "I play piano on Wednesday."
  5. Other students should listen and help out.
Basic Pair Practice
  1. Give each student a card.
  2. Display one of the weekdays.
  3. Students pair up and take turns saying, eg. "I play soccer on Tuesday."
  4. Students should then trade cards so that they can practice saying different activities.
Match Up
  1. Give each student a card.
  2. Display one of the weekdays.
  3. Students should remember the activity shown on their card for that weekday.
  4. Then, leaving their schedule face-down on their desks, students should pair up and take turns asking, and answering, eg. "What do you do on Friday?"
  5. Rather than trading cards, each student is now looking for another student who does the sam activity on that weekday.
  6. When they find such a student, they should report to the teacher.
*Only print enough pages for each student to get one card. The number of pages you print will be the number of students in the class who have the same activity for any given weekday. If you print a larger set of cards to use in a smaller class, just make sure you use them in groups of 6 indicated by a small period appearing at the bottom left of the same weekday.
