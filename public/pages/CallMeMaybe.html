CALL ME MAYBE

This is a listening exercise for students learning numbers 1~10

  1. Print only enough pages for each student to get one card.
  2. Give each student a random card and have them stand up.
  3. Tell students that, as soon as the number on their card is different than the one they hear, they should sit down.
  4. Choose one phone number and call it out slowly one digit at a time.
  5. When you're finished calling the number, there should be only one student still standing.
  6. They should answer the phone by saying, "Hello?"
  7. Continue the conversation however you like.
