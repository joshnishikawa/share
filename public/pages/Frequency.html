FREQUENCY
Print A4 in landscape orientation.

This is a group activity for practicing adverbs of frequency (always, usually, sometimes, never) with daily routines and activities.

  1. Hand out one worksheet to each group of 3 or 4 students.
  2. Students should cut out the 4 activities on the bottom.
  3. They should then discuss how to arrange them to complete the sentences on the top.
  4. Encourage them to come up with some funny ones. (It's supposed to get a laugh.)
  5. Have the students share their sentences with the class either individually or as a group.
