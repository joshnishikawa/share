DAILY SCHEDULE

An activity for practicing daily activities and times. These cards can be used in various ways.

Basic Group Practice
  1. Get into groups of 4 or 5.
  2. Give each student a card with a schedule.
  3. Display one of the daily activities.
  4. Each student in the group should take a turn saying the time of that activity shown on their card.
  5. Other students should listen and help out.
Basic Pair Practice
  1. Give each student a card.
  2. Display one of the daily activities.
  3. Students pair up and take turns saying, eg. "I get up at 6:15."
  4. Students should then trade cards so that they can practice saying different times.
Match Up
  1. Give each student a card.
  2. Display one of the daily activities.
  3. Students should remember the time shown on their card for that activity.
  4. Then, leaving their schedule face-down on their desks, students should pair up and take turns asking, and answering, eg. "What time do you go to bed?"
  5. Rather than trading cards, each student is now looking for another student who does that activity at the same time they do.
  6. When they find such a student, they should report to the teacher.
*Only print enough pages for each student to get one card. The number of pages you print will be the number of students in the class who have the same time for any given activity. If you print a larger set of cards to use in a smaller class, just make sure you use them in groups of 6 indicated by a small period appearing at the bottom left of the same activity.
